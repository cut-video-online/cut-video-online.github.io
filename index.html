Frequently asked questions

Is it free to cut video online here?

Yes. Vidione has a free tier that lets you trim, crop and export short clips, and nothing needs to be installed. Paid plans add longer videos, 1080p export and no watermark.

Which file formats can I upload?

MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and AVI are all accepted, and the export is MP4 by default so it plays everywhere.

Does the YouTube clip cutter download the whole video?

No. You paste the link, pick the segment and only that part is processed and exported, which keeps it fast even for long streams.

Will cutting the video reduce its quality?

Trimming keeps the original resolution and bitrate. Cropping re-encodes the frame, and you choose the output resolution up to 1080p.

Can I cut video online on my phone?

Yes. The editor works in a mobile browser, so you can trim a clip on iPhone or Android without installing an app.

Is there a length limit?

The free tier is designed for short clips. Paid plans raise the limit so you can cut full podcast episodes or hour-long recordings.
